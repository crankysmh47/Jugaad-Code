  • Lock workspace protection
  • Force immediate git checkpoint
  • Prevent starting long-running commands

About the Project

The story, philosophy, and mission behind building a Pakistan-first developer resilience layer.

☪

The Philosophy of Jugaad

In Pakistan, "Jugaad" is more than just a word — it’s a mindset of resourceful problem-solving, resilience, and ingenuity. When the electricity grid drops, when submarine internet cables break in the Arabian Sea, and when ISPs throttle international bandwidth, Pakistani engineers don't stop building. They adapt.

jugaad-code was created to give AI-assisted development tools like Claude Code the same street-smart resilience that Pakistani developers have had to master for decades.

💡

Why It Matters

A single sudden power cut during a complex multi-file refactor can wipe hours of unsaved context. jugaad-code turns your machine into a self-preserving fortress with automated git checkpoints and mirror failovers.

🔒

100% Local & Private

Runs purely on your local machine with zero external cloud telemetry. All health checks, ISP detection fallbacks, power monitoring, and state caches operate in under 5ms without slowing down tool execution.

🌍

Built for Pakistan, Ready for the World

While engineered around Pakistani infrastructure (PTCL, StormFiber, SMW4, load-shedding), the same chaos affects developers across South Asia, Africa, and Latin America. jugaad-code proves how AI tooling should adapt to real-world conditions globally.
